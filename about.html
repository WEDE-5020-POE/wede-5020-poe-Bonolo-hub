About Us

Alpha Group Security (Pty) Ltd is a small but growing private security company based in Bloemfontein, Free State. The company focuses on providing reliable, professional, and affordable security services to residential, commercial, and event-based clients. As a newly established business, Alpha Group Security aims to build trust within the local community by delivering high quality services supported by trained personnel and modern security solutions.

Companies Mission

To provide professional, reliable, and affordable security services

Vision

To become a trusted and recognized security service provider.

Company Values

Driven by integrity, vigilance, and professionalism, Alpha Group Security focuses on delivering tailored security solutions that respond to the unique needs of each client. The company prioritises continuous training, operational discipline, and client satisfaction to ensure consistent, high-quality service delivery across South Africa.
