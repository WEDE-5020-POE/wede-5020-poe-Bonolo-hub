Who We Are

Alpha Group Security (Pty) Ltd is a small but growing private security company based in Bloemfontein, Free State. The company focuses on providing reliable, professional, and affordable security services to residential, commercial, and event-based clients.

As a newly established business, Alpha Group Security aims to build trust within the local community by delivering high quality services supported by trained personnel and modern security solutions.
